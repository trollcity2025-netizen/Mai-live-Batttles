MAI CORP · HOW MAI LIFE WARS BATTLES WORK

Choose your format. Own your moment.

Understand standard, fast, slow, state, and team battles in MAI Life Wars.

Choose a format from 1v1 to 6v6. Standard battles show a three-minute countdown at normal speed.

Fast Mode displays 3:00 but counts down twice as fast for a 90-second real-time round. Slow Mode displays 3:00 but counts down at half speed for a six-minute real-time round.

State Battles show a participant’s chosen state. Battle results and scores are validated by the server. The initial product has no sudden death and no rematch flow.
