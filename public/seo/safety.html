Safety belongs in every round

Learn about AI-assisted audio moderation, threats, self-harm safety, sexual content rules, and reporting.

Mai Live Wars uses automated moderation to help detect threats of violence, self-harm content, and prohibited sexual content during live battles.

Confirmed high-confidence violations can remove a participant from a battle. In 1v1, the eligible opponent can receive a forfeit win. In team formats, only the offending participant is removed while the remaining battle continues when possible.

Automated moderation can make mistakes. Mai Live Wars must use context checks, reliable speaker attribution, incident review, and appropriate safety fallbacks.
